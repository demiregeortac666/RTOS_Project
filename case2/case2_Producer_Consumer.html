Policy Trade-offs

🔒
Blocked (RTOS Semaphore): Zero data loss — every message is guaranteed to be consumed. Trade-off: Producer latency increases when the queue is full; the system may stall under heavy load.
↺
Overwrite (Ring Buffer): Always stays current — Producer never stalls. Trade-off: Old messages are silently overwritten; historical data is lost if the Consumer is too slow.
